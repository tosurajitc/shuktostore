📋 Template 01 — Client Onboarding Brief
▼ How to use this template

What this template is for

  • Use this at the start of every new client engagement — before or immediately after the kickoff call.
  • It captures all the critical information in one professional 2-page document that both you and the client sign off on.
  • Doubles as a scope reference during delivery — refer back to it if scope creep occurs.

How to fill it in

SectionWhat to enterWhen
Agency Name (header)Your AI agency's trading nameOnce — then keep as default
01 — Client InformationCompany name, contact, email, phone, website, time zoneDuring or just after kickoff call
02 — Business SnapshotWhat they do, who their customer is, team size, tech stackDuring discovery call
03 — Goals & MetricsPrimary goal, KPIs, timeline, budgetDuring discovery call
04 — Scope of Work tableEach deliverable, who owns it, due dateAfter scoping session
05 — Onboarding ChecklistTick each box as steps are completedOngoing during onboarding week
06 — Risks & NotesAny blockers, out-of-scope items, dependenciesBefore sending to client
07 — SignaturesPrint → sign physically, or use a tool like DocuSign / HelloSignBefore work begins

Step-by-step workflow

  • Step 1 — Duplicate this file. Rename it: onboarding-ClientName-2025.html
  • Step 2 — Fill in all dashed fields (click to edit). Start with the header (agency name, date, project ID).
  • Step 3 — Work through Sections 01–06 using your discovery call notes.
  • Step 4 — Click "Print / Save as PDF". Set destination to Save as PDF in Chrome. A4 portrait.
  • Step 5 — Send to client for review and signature before starting any work.
  • Step 6 — File the signed PDF in your client folder. Use it as your scope reference throughout delivery.
💡 Pro tip: Keep one master blank copy of this file as your agency default. Duplicate it fresh for every new client so you never accidentally overwrite a previous brief.
