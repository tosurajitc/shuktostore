📊 Template 04 — Project Dashboard
▼ How to use this template

What this template is for

  • A client-facing weekly status report — safe to share directly with clients without exposing internal ops details.
  • Use one copy per project per reporting period. Create a new copy each week or bi-weekly.
  • Keeps clients informed, reduces "where are we?" check-in calls, and documents your delivery track record.

Weekly reporting workflow

  • Step 1 — Duplicate the file at the start of each reporting week. Rename: dashboard-ClientName-Week3.html
  • Step 2 — Update the KPI strip first — overall progress %, days elapsed, budget, blockers.
  • Step 3 — Click deliverable status pills to update them; add notes for anything completed or changed.
  • Step 4 — Click each milestone's dot to advance it: Upcoming → Active → Done.
  • Step 5 — Replace Section 05 actions entirely with this week's specifics.
  • Step 6 — Print to PDF and email to client, or paste the link in your shared project channel.
💡 Pro tip: The status pill in the header (On Track) is the first thing clients see. Change it honestly — clients prefer a flagged risk over a surprise at go-live.
