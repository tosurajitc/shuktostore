Set up in 4 steps

1

Click "Open in Notion & Duplicate" above

Notion will show you a preview of the template. Click the "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner. It copies the whole system into your own Notion workspace.

2

Go to the 👋 Start Here page first

Fill in your household name, monthly budget, and family members. Add names, ages and roles — these flow through to Chores, Money and other sections automatically.

3

Fill in your Fixed Monthly Commitments

This is the most important 5 minutes. Add your home loan EMI, car loan, SIPs, insurance premiums and any other fixed payments. You'll see exactly how much of your income is already committed before you spend anything.

4

Check your 🏠 Dashboard

Your dashboard shows spending, upcoming bills, pending chores, low stock and things that need attention — all live. Replace the sample data with yours whenever you're ready. There's no rush.

💛 A few tips to get the most out of it

• You don't have to fill everything in on day one. Start with Bills and Money — those two sections alone are worth the price.
• The sample data is there to show you how things work — delete or replace it whenever you like.
• Add the Notion app to your phone. The Shopping List and Chores tabs are most useful when you can update them on the go.
• Share the workspace with your partner or family — Notion's free plan allows limited sharing so everyone can see and update the same system.
• Try the 🤖 AI Assistant section — copy your spending or chores into ChatGPT using the ready-made prompts and let it help you plan the week.
