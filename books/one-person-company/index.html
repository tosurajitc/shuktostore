"Starting a company used to require a team. AI is changing who can do what — but most of the writing about it is either hype or too technical to act on."

The gap this book fills

There's no shortage of AI news. What's missing is a clear, honest framework for a non-technical person to look at their own business — or the business they want to build — and understand exactly where AI agents help, where they don't, and how to think about introducing them.

This book follows Maya, a fictional consultant, building an AI-augmented business from scratch — chapter by chapter, function by function. Real decisions, real trade-offs, no magic.

Every concept becomes a framework. Every framework comes with a worksheet. The final chapter is a 30-day action plan you can start the moment you close the book.
