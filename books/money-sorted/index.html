Baseline method

"Every money book assumes you get paid the same amount every month. You don't. That's not a flaw — it just means you need different advice."

The problem with standard finance advice

The classic advice — save 20%, max your 401k, follow the 50/30/20 rule — is designed for someone with a predictable monthly salary. If you're a freelancer, gig worker, or anyone with variable income, that advice doesn't just fail to help. It actively misleads.

This book is built from the ground up for how irregular income actually works — including how to budget when you don't know what next month looks like.

The promise

A concrete system for money — no matter what your income looks like this month.

Real percentages, real worksheets, a 30-Day Money Reset, and no vague encouragement. Every chapter ends with something you can do this week.
